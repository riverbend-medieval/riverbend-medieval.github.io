What We Do

Riverbend Medieval Society runs regular activities that combine historical research, practical skill-building, and public engagement. Members participate at their own pace, from beginner-friendly workshops through to combat displays and event encampments.

Core Activities

Combat Training

Structured drilling and sparring for safe, controlled medieval combat presentation using period-inspired weapons and tactics.

View Combat Training Activity Gallery →

Archery

Target practice, range safety, and historical archery skill development with a focus on technique and consistency.

View Archery Activity Gallery →

Medieval Crafts

Hands-on craft sessions including clothing, leatherwork, woodwork, camp equipment, and other practical period skills.

View Medieval Crafts Activity Gallery →

Persona Development

Research and storytelling workshops to help members build authentic characters with coherent historical backgrounds.

Workshops and Event Preparation

Weekend workshops and planning sessions to prepare displays, camps, and demonstrations for public events.

Medieval Cooking

Camp kitchen demonstrations, baking, roasting, and period-inspired feast preparation.

View Cooking Activity Gallery →

Public Demonstrations

Community-facing displays at festivals and fairs where we share medieval combat, crafts, and daily life with visitors.

Get Involved
