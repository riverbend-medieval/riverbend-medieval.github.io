About Riverbend Medieval Society

Riverbend Medieval Society Inc. is a not-for-profit community dedicated to bringing medieval history to life through authentic re-enactment on the Fraser Coast, Queensland. Members research, train, and present the culture, crafts, and martial traditions of England between 800 and 1500 AD.

Our purpose

  1. To be a not-for-profit organisation promoting social and physical enactment for people and families interested in English historical life from 800 to 1500 AD.
  2. To promote and encourage tourism to the Fraser Coast.

The society is comprised of three companies:

Our Companies

Sevenoaks Early Medieval Company
850–950 AD
The High Medieval Company
1100–1300 AD
The Companie of the Badger
1350–1450 AD
